fix: readable in a dark theme, and no Settings surface

Two things from the screenshots of the inline bar:

- the text was invisible until selected. DSH 0.2 does not ship
  `--dsw-alias-bg-elevated`, so the bar's surface fell back to `#f7f7f8` while
  its text took `--dsw-alias-label-primary` - light on light in a dark theme.
  Floating surfaces now fall back through the layered tokens 0.2 does ship
  (bg-elevated -> bg-layer-2 -> bg-base), and text, hairlines and the tick's ring
  fall back to `inherit` / `currentColor` instead of assuming a light theme. The
  browser fixture now runs on dark tokens WITHOUT bg-elevated and asserts the
  text/surface lightness distance, so this cannot come back silently.
- the Settings contributions are gone, as asked: no row in General, no card under
  Plugins. Switching modes stays on the entry button (left click = the stored
  mode, right click = the other one), in the inline bar, and in the panel.
